# Country-by-country dish research

Research date: September 30, 2026. **195 entries, matching every country/territory label returned by the current MealDB area list.**

These are proposed **iconic or traditional starter dishes**, not a verified ranking of the most popular dish in each country. Sources establish a dish's local association or culinary significance; they do not use one shared measure of popularity. One candidate per entry keeps the first catalog manageable. Regional variants and shared dishes are intentional. Some candidates are staples, desserts, or special-occasion preparations rather than quick dinners.

Coverage comes from the [live MealDB area list](https://www.themealdb.com/api/json/v1/1/list.php?a=list). The list includes territories; its labels are retained here to match the API, not to classify sovereignty. Every row has a food source. Government/tourism and cultural sources are used where available, supplemented by food publications and encyclopedic references. This is desk research and requires recipe/editorial review before publication.

**Recipe availability is unverified for all rows.** These are not confirmed MealDB matches or ready-to-publish recipes. A country in the area list can still lack the selected dish. Cultural research sources are separate from recipe and photo licensing. No ranking scores, cooking times, or recipes have been invented.
